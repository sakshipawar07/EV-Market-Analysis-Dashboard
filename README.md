# EV Market Analysis Dashboard — India #

An interactive **Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Analysis Dashboard** focused on the Indian EV market from **2015–2024**.

The dashboard provides insights into EV sales, vehicle categories, manufacturers, and state-wise operational charging infrastructure.

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard analyzes the growth of the Electric Vehicle market in India and presents key trends through interactive visualizations.

It covers:

- Year-wise EV sales
- EV sales by vehicle category
- State-wise operational charging infrastructure
- Top EV manufacturers
- Category-wise EV sales
- Average EV sales
- Key market insights

The dashboard is designed to make EV market trends easier to understand through an interactive and visually appealing interface.

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 1. Average Yearly Sales

Shows the trend in EV sales from **2015 to 2024**, highlighting the rapid growth of the Indian EV market in recent years.

### 2. EV Sales by Category

Breaks down EV sales across different vehicle categories:

- 2W — Two Wheelers
- 3W — Three Wheelers
- LMV — Light Motor Vehicles
- MMV — Medium Motor Vehicles

### 3. Top 5 States by Operational PCS

Displays the states with the highest number of operational **Public Charging Stations (PCS)**.

The dashboard highlights:

- Maharashtra
- Delhi
- Karnataka
- Kerala
- Tamil Nadu

### 4. Category-wise Sales

Compares total sales across different EV categories using a horizontal bar chart.

### 5. Top 7 EV Manufacturers

Shows the leading EV manufacturers based on sales/registrations in the dataset.

### 6. Average EV Sales

A gauge visualization displaying the average EV sales metric.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- EV sales show significant growth toward the later years of the 2015–2024 period.
- Two-wheelers represent a major share of EV sales.
- Three-wheelers also account for a substantial portion of total EV sales.
- Maharashtra has the highest operational charging infrastructure among the states displayed.
- Ola Electric Technologies appears among the leading manufacturers in the dashboard dataset.
